Attack Tree Decomposition & Scam Taxonomy

How Verix AI maps social engineering scripts into actionable on-device sensory vector signals.

1. "Digital Arrest" & Legal Impersonation

Criminals impersonating CBI, Police, or ED, accusing victims of money laundering and coercing immediate transfers.

  • Stage 1: Cold Call: +91 22... TelecomManager OFFHOOK ➔ ACTIVE_CALL=1
  • Stage 2: Authority Lure: "CBI Crime Branch" ➔ AUTHORITY_IMPERSONATION (w=14)
  • Stage 3: Isolation: "Do not disconnect, go to private room" ➔ VICTIM_ISOLATION (w=13)
  • Stage 4: Coerced Intent: ₹48,000 to new VPA ➔ AMT_SPIKE_RATIO + NEW_VPA
  • Mitigation: Synergy Multiplier (+0.50) ➔ Tier 3 Critical Block (5s)
2. "Enter PIN to Receive Money" Trap

OLX marketplace / cashback scams tricking victims into believing entering their UPI PIN receives a refund.

  • Stage 1: Buyer Lure: Fraudster claims to pay advance for marketplace item.
  • Stage 2: Collect Request Intent: Sends UPI collect request or intent link.
  • Stage 3: Verbal Coercion: "Enter PIN for refund" ➔ PIN_REFUND_TRAP (w=12)
  • Stage 4: Cold Payee: Unseen merchant VPA ➔ NEW_VPA_COLD_START (w=6)
  • Mitigation: Synergy Rule PINTRAP ∧ NEW_VPA ➔ Tier 3 Alert
3. AnyDesk / Remote Access Fraud

Victim coerced to install AnyDesk/TeamViewer under guise of bank customer support or KYC assistance.

  • Stage 1: Fake Bank Support: Fraudulent Google Maps / Twitter support number.
  • Stage 2: Sideload Request: "Install AnyDesk" ➔ APK_SIDELOAD_PRESSURE (w=11)

System Architecture & Mathematical Specifications

Formal definition of the Zero-Cloud On-Device Risk Formulation, 3-Tier Cognitive Friction, and Weight Tuning Loop.

1. Risk Index Formulation

The composite Risk Index $R \in [0, 100]$ is computed deterministically in under 4.2 milliseconds:

R = min(100, (100 · S_raw · M) / S_max)

where S_raw = ∑(w_i · v_i) for i ∈ [1, 19]
and M = 1 + ∑(γ_k · I_k)

Where $S_{max} = 190$ represents theoretical maximum saturation, $v_i \in \{0, 1\}$ is the signal presence, $w_i$ are calibrated weights, and $\gamma_k$ are nonlinear synergy multipliers for intersecting attack vectors.

2. 3-Tier Adaptive Cognitive Friction

Friction dynamically scales based on explainable risk classification:

Tier 1: Safe Pass-Through (R < 30)
Zero cognitive load. 800ms subtle green banner. Instant transition to UPI PIN.
Tier 2: Advisory Intercept (30 ≤ R < 70)
Amber modal showing plain-language anomaly explanations. One-click dismissible.
Tier 3: Critical Circuit Breaker (R ≥ 70)
Red full-screen intercept. Prominent "Hang Up & Cancel" primary CTA. Mandatory checkbox + 5-second countdown delay on secondary override.
3. Zero-PII Telemetry Architecture

To respect user privacy and comply with Indian DPDP Act 2023, audio streams and raw phone numbers never leave the device.

  • Audio: Streamed into on-device memory buffer, scanned with regex, instantly garbage collected.
  • Identifiers: Device ID and VPA are one-way hashed with salted HMAC-SHA256.
  • Amount: Quantized into coarse logarithmic buckets (e.g., 10k-50k) before wire sync.
